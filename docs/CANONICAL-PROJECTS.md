**Status:** Real R Squared AI engagement. Publish anonymized per privacy rules.

**Publishable framing:** "An MGU within the Alliant Insurance ecosystem. Delivered through R Squared AI."

**Category:** Custom CRM / AI Document Processing / Insurance

**Summary:** AI engine that reads broker insurance submissions (7–15 documents per submission), extracts structured data using dual competing AI models (Claude Opus + GPT cross-validation), validates, and auto-populates Salesforce records — replacing manual data entry across disconnected systems where the same data was entered 3–5 times.

**What existed before:** ~1,700 submissions/year across WC, GL, Commercial Auto, Excess Liability. Same data entered 3–5 times across Salesforce, Majesco/Coverall, Chubb systems, Excel pricing models, and Epic billing. With two new carrier markets (AXA XL, Everest) alongside Chubb, needed to scale to ~2,500/year without adding headcount. ~70% of submissions declined at various stages.

**What was built:** Email intake → document classification → AI extraction per JSON schema mapped to Salesforce objects → dual-model cross-validation (fields <0.90 confidence trigger second model) → cross-document validation → Cosmos DB → PubSub sync back to Salesforce → human-in-the-loop review for low-confidence items in custom Salesforce interfaces.

**Specifications:** Architecture: Salesforce → PubSub → Azure AI Foundry (Claude Opus primary + GPT cross-validation) → Cosmos DB → Salesforce. Custom Salesforce objects: Astrus_Submission__c, Line_Of_Business__c, Astrus_LOB_Quote__c, Astrus_Submission_Quote__c, Submission Communication. Document types: ACORD 125/126/127/137/130/131, vehicle schedules (Excel), loss runs (PDF/Excel from 40–50 carrier formats), financials, broker narratives. 0.90 confidence threshold for dual-model validation. Lines: WC, GL, Commercial Auto, Umbrella/Excess.

**Personnel (PRIVATE — do not publish):** Joan Miles, Pat Murphy, Nicole Brown/Hansen, Jeffrey Hall, Rich Kearns (client side); Rich Rivara, Will Godfrey, Josh Eliason, Tyler Preisser (R Squared side).

**Results (publishable):** Zero missed renewals in first 6 months. Commission reconciliation under 30 min/month. Real-time book visibility for the principal.
